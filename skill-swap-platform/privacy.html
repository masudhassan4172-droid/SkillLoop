SkillLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

SkillLoop collects the email address used for authentication and the profile information you choose to publish: name, programme, skills, and bio. This information is used only to operate the skill-swap community.

Your profile is visible to people using SkillLoop. Swap requests are visible only to the sender and recipient. We do not sell personal information.

You can edit your profile at any time. To delete your account and related profile and request data, sign in, open My profile, and select Delete account.
